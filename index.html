Project information

How it works

Four simple steps from account details to a prediction.

1. Enter details

Type the username, followers, following, posts, account age, and engagement numbers.

2. Validate & process

Inputs are checked, then ratios such as followers-to-following and engagement are calculated.

3. Model predicts

A trained Random Forest of {{ stats.trees or 200 }} decision trees votes on genuine or fake.

4. Read the result

See the prediction, probability, key indicators and a short explanation.
